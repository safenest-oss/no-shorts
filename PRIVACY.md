# Privacy Policy – No Shorts

_Last updated: 2026-10-28_

No Shorts is a browser extension that hides YouTube Shorts. It is part of [SafeNest](https://github.com/safenest-oss), a collection of small open source tools that make the Internet safer for children.

## Short version

**No Shorts does not collect, store, share or transmit any data.**

## What the extension does on your device

- It runs only on `youtube.com` pages.
- It changes how the page looks (hides Shorts) and redirects `youtube.com/shorts/<ID>` links to the regular video page.
- It does this locally in your browser, by reading the page you are already viewing.

## What it does not do

- No personal data, browsing history, search queries, account details or video information is collected or stored.
- No analytics, telemetry, cookies, advertising or tracking.
- No network requests. The extension never contacts any server, including ours.
- No remote code. All code ships inside the extension and is open source.
- No settings or data are stored anywhere, not even on your device.

## Permissions

The extension requests no special browser permissions. It only declares that its content script runs on `youtube.com` (`www.youtube.com` and `m.youtube.com`), which is required to hide Shorts on those pages.

## Children

The extension is designed to be used on devices of children and does not collect any information from them or from their parents.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published in this repository with an updated date. Since the extension collects no data, any change that would introduce data collection would be a breaking change to the project's [privacy principles](https://github.com/safenest-oss/.github/blob/main/PRIVACY_PRINCIPLES.md) and would be discussed publicly first.

## Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at <https://github.com/safenest-oss/no-shorts/issues>.
