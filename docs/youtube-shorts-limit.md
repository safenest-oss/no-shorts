# Limit Shorts on phones (YouTube + Family Link)

No Shorts only works in desktop browsers. Chrome on Android and iOS and the YouTube app have no extensions. On phones, use YouTube's own supervised-account limit. Polish version for parents: [youtube-shorts-limit.pl.md](youtube-shorts-limit.pl.md).

## Steps (Family Link app on the parent's phone)

1. Open **Family Link** and pick the child's profile.
2. **Controls** → **YouTube**.
3. **Time Management** → **Daily Limits**.
4. **Shorts Feed** → set **0 minutes**.

## What we verified (2026-11-09)

- It worked on the account of a **child under 13**.
- The child **cannot change** the limit while signed in to YouTube on their own supervised Google account.

## Condition

It only protects when the child uses their **own Google account everywhere** (YouTube app, Android devices, browsers). On a parent's account or a shared signed-out device there is no control.

## What we do not know

- Whether the limit also applies to YouTube in a desktop browser (not tested).
- It is a time limit, not removal of Shorts.
- This is a Google feature and may change. Open an [issue](../../../issues) if it looks different for you.
